**How I Work**

As an AI enthusiast, Claude and ChatGPT are part of how I build software:

1. Before starting a project, I talk through the idea, tech stack, and workflow with both, then have them each put together a plan.
2. I compare the two plans and go with whichever works best, sometimes one, sometimes the other, sometimes a combination of both.
3. I have Claude execute the plan to build out the app framework, then make my own changes.
4. When the app is finished, I have Claude and ChatGPT each review it and generate first-pass documentation, everything from the README to setup instructions.
5. I compare the two and go with whichever is better, or combine the strongest parts of both.
6. I check that it actually matches how the app behaves, fill in anything missed, and rewrite it in my own voice before it's final.
